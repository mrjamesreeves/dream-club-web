Dream Club

A dignified home for your dreams.

Download for iPhone & Mac

No ads or subscriptions. Ever.

Why

A dream lasts about ninety seconds after you open your eyes.

Dream Club is built for those ninety seconds. Open it half-asleep, type or talk until you run out, and put the phone down. Everything else — the title, the symbols, any interpretation — waits until you're awake enough to care.

The Mac app is the other half of the ritual: a proper desk for rereading, editing, and watching your own motifs pile up over months.

What it does

Capture

Type it or speak it, in the dark, with one hand. Voice notes are transcribed on your device. Nothing is asked of you but the dream itself.

Reflect

When you want a second reading, ask for one: Jungian, Lacanian, or Deleuzian. Never automatic, never unbidden. A mirror, not a fortune teller.

Share

Invite up to twelve people you actually know. When your dream and a friend's share a symbol in the same week, you'll a synchronicity alert.

Details
  • iPhone, iPad, and Mac — one free download
  • Syncs through your own iCloud, not our servers
  • Voice capture with on-device transcription
  • Three analytical lenses, only when asked
  • Symbols and themes that accumulate into an inventory
  • Private circles of up to twelve people
  • Alerts when a symbol crosses between dreams
  • Attach a photograph to any dream
  • Full-text search across everything you've written
  • Editable dates for transcribing old paper journals
  • Dreams are numbered, oldest to newest
  • Sign in with Apple, or don't sign in at all
Privacy

Your journal is nobody else's business.

Dreams live on your device and in your own iCloud. Nothing leaves unless you deliberately share a single dream with your circle — and you can unshare it just as easily. There is no public feed, no discovery, no strangers.

No ads, no trackers, no analytics, nothing to sell. Signing in is optional: the journal and the analysis work without an account. Read the privacy policy →
