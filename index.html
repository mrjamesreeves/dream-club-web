Dream Club

A dignified home for your dreams.

Here in the Middle West of the United States, we meet every month to discuss the strange movies in our heads when we’re free from ego and intention.

We built Dream Club to make it easier to collect, analyze, and share our dreams.

Download for iPhone & Mac

Free for 30 days, then $10 to unlock it forever on all devices with ongoing updates.

Private by Design

No ads. No trackers. No feeds or strangers. Your journal never touches our servers unless you request an analysis (sent anonymously, never stored) or share a dream with your circle of friends.

Signing in with your Apple ID is optional: the journal and the analysis work without an account. Read our privacy policy →

  • Log your dreams while half-awake via text or voice.
  • Analyze your dreams through three lenses: Jungian, Lacanian, and Deleuzian.
  • Share a dream with a circle of friends and receive synchronicity alerts.
  • A companion desktop app for organizing, searching, and editing.
  • No subscriptions. No accounts.

Journal

An archive of your dreaming life with seasonal reports. Every dream stays on your device and in your iCloud—synced across your devices, safe through reinstalls, readable only by you unless you choose to share it.

Analysis

Get a different perspective on your dreams through three analytical lenses:

  • Jungian: archetypes, the shadow, the collective unconscious.
  • Lacanian: desire, language, what resists saying.
  • Deleuzian: flows, thresholds, becoming.

Symbols

Every dream’s key images—fire, staircases, teeth—become part of a personal codex of people, places, and ideas. A mythology will surface.

Club

Totally optional: Create a private, invite-only group of up to twelve people you know and share a dream. If a friend’s dream shares the same symbols, Dream Club delivers a synchronicity alert.
